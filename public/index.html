The suggestion box

Run by a local, for local restaurants.

This is not a national software company with a support ticket queue. It is run by a neighbor who eats in these restaurants, drinks at these bars, and answers their own email. When something needs adjusting, you talk to the person who runs it.

The game library

Nobody plans the twenty minutes a table spends waiting on the kitchen. Here is what is on the tables now, and we are always working on more.

Groups

Who Knows Who

One player answers a question about themselves and the table guesses what they picked. Two turns each, two to eight players.

Whole table

Guess the Split

Vote on a two way question, then guess what share of all diners picked the same. Solo, or up to eight players.

Social deduction

Who Invited You?

Everyone gets the same secret word except one player, who gets only the category and has to bluff. Three to eight players.

Solo friendly

Wordy

A quick guess the word puzzle somebody can play alone, without waiting on the rest of the table.

Competitive

Trivia

Fast question rounds at pub difficulty with the answer revealed the second you pick. Solo, or head to head up to eight.

Bid and prove

All Talk

Bid on how many answers in a category you can rattle off in thirty seconds, then the top bidder proves it out loud. Two to eight players.

Teams

Cornhole

The backyard classic on the phone, with your restaurant's logo on the board. Two taps to throw, red versus blue in teams of one or two, or solo practice.

Beat the clock

Quick Pour

Three beer taps, ten glasses, one clock, and your restaurant's logo etched on every glass. Fill each one to the brass line without spilling the foam. Fastest time wins the table.

Just for fun

Fortune Teller

Three cards face down in your colors. Pick one for a quick fortune while the kitchen works.

Everything runs in your guest's own browser. No app to download, no tablet, nothing for your staff to explain. New questions go in constantly, and new games get added over time.

How it helps your restaurant

A better guest experience

Guest experience is the part everyone competes on and nobody can buy. A table playing something together is a table enjoying the night, and that is what people remember and repeat. It happens at every table, every service, without a server having to do anything.

You hear the complaints first

The suggestion box is anonymous, so people are honest. The music is too loud, the menu is confusing, the bathroom needs attention. You read it in private while you can still fix it, instead of in a public review two weeks later. Asking also says something on its own: guests notice a restaurant that wants to know.

Your own line to every table

Post a message and it shows up on the first screen your guests see. Live music Friday. The new brunch. Add a link and it becomes a button to your menu, tickets, or Instagram. No algorithm decides who sees it: this is on your own tables, in front of guests already sitting in your restaurant, and you can change it in seconds.

More Google reviews

Everyone who uses the card sees the same simple, neutral prompt. More of your happy guests actually reach the review page instead of just meaning to.
